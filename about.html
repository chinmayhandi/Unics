About Unics Coaching

Empowering students with industry-relevant computer skills since 2015.

Our Story

Unics Computer Coaching was founded with a single vision: to bridge the gap between academic education and industry requirements. We noticed that many students lacked the practical skills needed to thrive in the modern tech landscape.

Over the years, we have trained thousands of students, helping them secure their dream jobs through our rigorous, hands-on training approach and state-of-the-art infrastructure.

  • ISO 9001:2015 Certified Institute
  • Modern Computer Labs
  • 100% Placement Assistance

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative tech education center, globally recognized for producing highly skilled and employable professionals.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality computer education through practical training, expert mentorship, and industry-aligned curriculum.
